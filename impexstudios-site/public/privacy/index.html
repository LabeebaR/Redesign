Privacy policy

How Impex Studios collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What we collect

When you contact us or request a growth plan, we collect the details you submit: your name, business name, email, phone number, website and message. Our site may also use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are visited.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, prepare your growth plan or proposal, and deliver services you’ve agreed to. We do not sell your information. We will only send you marketing messages if you’ve given consent, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our website, forms, email and CRM. They process data on our behalf and only as needed to provide those services. Some providers may store data outside Canada.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing contact@impexstudios.com.
