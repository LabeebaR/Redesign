Frequently asked questions

Will callers know they’re talking to AI?

We recommend a short disclosure at the start of each call. It builds trust and is expected under many privacy and telemarketing rules. The agent sounds natural and can transfer to a person any time.

It depends on call volume and integrations. You’ll get a fixed setup price and a clear monthly estimate in your growth plan. A website chatbot can be added to any site package for $299.

Can it book into my calendar?

Yes. We connect Google Calendar, Outlook, GoHighLevel and most booking tools, and the agent only offers times you’re actually available.
