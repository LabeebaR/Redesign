Pricing questions

Are these prices final?

Yes. Your growth plan lists the exact scope and a fixed price. If you ask for something outside that scope, we quote it separately before doing it.

How does payment work?

50% to start your project and 50% when you approve the finished site on staging. Nothing goes live until you’re happy.

What about monthly services?

SEO, social media, AI phone agents and automation are quoted monthly in your growth plan based on scope. No long lock-in contracts.

Is hosting included?

We deploy on fast, secure hosting and walk you through the options. Most small sites run on low-cost or free hosting tiers.

Do you offer payment plans?

Ask us. For larger builds we can split payments across milestones.
